Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Dahshat Bot is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data we access

The app uses Google OAuth only to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. It does not access data of any other users.

Data storage and sharing

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal data with third parties. OAuth tokens are stored privately by the owner and are used only to perform uploads.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
